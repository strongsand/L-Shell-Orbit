# L-Shell Orbit 0.2.0-beta.2

This version brings the next branch improvements to everyday Starlink monitoring.

- Current readings in metric details, kept separate from the selected historical period.
- PoE input voltage from compatible routers, with terminal association and freshness checks.
- Reorderable cards, adaptive sizes, mini charts and refinements to navigation and AMOLED mode.
- Improvements to history, occurrences and daily, weekly and monthly reports.
- Manual backup and restore of local data.
- Refined normal and extreme tests, scheduled and smart tests, and result screens.
- Widget, notification and Portuguese, English and Spanish text improvements.
- Optional Power Monitor for voltage, current, power and net energy received from a compatible Beacon, with session charts and clear simulated-data labels.
- Compatibility fixes for different Android versions.

Power Monitor does not generate measurements on the phone, and Starlink monitoring does not require an electrical sensor. Beacon features depend on compatible firmware. AR shows predicted orbital positions; it does not identify the satellite serving the terminal. Android may delay background tasks.
