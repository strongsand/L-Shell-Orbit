English retains seven historical Portuguese fallback images. Its `4.png` has JPEG
content despite its filename. Spanish has no dedicated screenshot set. These
images are store-listing material, not a hardware compatibility certification.

For new captures, use consistent themes and readable text. Do not include private
notifications, credentials, pairing codes, precise locations or unnecessary
network/device identifiers. Keep simulated electrical readings clearly labeled.
Use the matching image extension and review the actual language before adding
captures to a locale's `images/phoneScreenshots/` directory.
